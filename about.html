About Me

The story behind my studies and my teaching internship.

I am Saddam Hussain Sahito, a BS English Language and Teaching student at the University of Sindh, Jamshoro, Pakistan, currently in my 6th semester. My interest in English language education, communication, and effective teaching has shaped my academic journey.

During my 82-hour teaching internship at Decent Public Higher Secondary School, Makli, Thatta, Sindh, Pakistan, from 17 September 2026 to 17 October 2026, I gained practical experience teaching English lessons, managing classroom activities, and evaluating students’ work. I worked with secondary (Grades 9–10) and higher secondary (Grades 11–12) levels.

These experiences have strengthened my understanding of classroom teaching and motivated me to continue developing my teaching skills.

My teaching philosophy

I believe that effective English language teaching should focus on developing students’ practical communication skills. My goal is to create engaging learning experiences that help students improve their speaking, listening, reading, and writing abilities. I believe that language learning becomes more meaningful when students can apply their knowledge confidently in real-life situations.
